AI at the Edge and Industrial Internet of Things

## Overview
This course explores the foundations and applied practices of Edge AI and the Industrial Internet of Things. The content progresses from development environments and machine learning workflows to networking, cloud integration, cybersecurity, autonomous agents, and advanced analytics. Each module builds practical and conceptual knowledge through labs, case studies, simulations, and technical reports.

The course emphasizes hands on learning, reproducible workflows, and real world applications of AI enabled IIoT systems.

---

# Course Structure

## Module 1  
Introduction to Edge AI and IIoT  
Focus on understanding the role of edge computing, IoT devices, and AI models in modern industrial systems.

## Module 2  
Development Environments and Tools  
Exploration of tools such as Visual Studio Code, Node.js, Edge Impulse CLI, TensorFlow, TensorFlow Lite, Google Colab, and generative AI coding assistants.

## Module 3  
Deploying AI Models on Simulated Edge Devices  
Training, converting, and deploying models using TensorFlow Lite and Edge Impulse.  
Emphasis on reproducible workflows and real time inference testing.

## Module 4  
IIoT Protocols and Sensor Network Simulation  
Simulation of MQTT, CoAP, and OPC UA sensor networks.  
Real time visualization and protocol comparison.

## Module 5  
Data Acquisition and Preprocessing  
Concepts of batch and real time data collection, sampling rates, filtering, aggregation, feature extraction, and data transformation.

## Module 6  
AI Driven Data Analytics with Nixtla  
Time series forecasting for IIoT data.  
Model selection, feature engineering, evaluation, and generative modeling.

## Module 7  
Age of Information and Reliability Trade Offs  
Analysis of AoI, packet loss probability, and IIoT network performance using machine learning and data exploration.

## Module 8  
Midterm Cybersecurity Project  
Design of a cybersecurity plan for an AI integrated IIoT system.  
Vulnerability assessment, defense strategy development, and penetration testing simulation.

## Module 9  
Autonomous Agents in Industry 4.0  
Case study analysis on reinforcement learning agents and their industrial applications.

## Module 10  
Cloud Integration for Edge and IIoT  
Cloud platforms, hybrid architectures, orchestration, and emerging trends such as AI optimized hardware and 6G.

## Module 11  
Capstone Preparation  
Integration of course concepts in preparation for the final project.

## Module 12  
AI Agents and Human Computer Interaction  
Exploration of AI agents, reinforcement learning, generative AI, robotics, and brain computer interfaces.
